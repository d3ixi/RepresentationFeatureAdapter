The Domain Is a Residue:

Adapting Self-Supervised Features, Not Generators

Thomas Deixelberger & Markus Steinberger
Huawei Technologies, Austria
Graz University of Technology, Austria
arXiv Code (coming soon)
TL;DR

In self-supervised features, fog, night and the look of a game engine are a residue, not the scene. We move that residue with a small adapter, never touching the generator, and still match or beat generators retrained from scratch.
